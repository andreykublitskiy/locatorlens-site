Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Andrey Kublitskiy (“we”) builds LocatorLens, a Chrome DevTools extension that generates and verifies XPath and CSS locators.

What the extension does

LocatorLens generates, ranks, and verifies CSS, XPath, and Playwright locators for the element selected in DevTools. It does not record tests, inject ads, or change the pages you visit except for a temporary outline overlay while DevTools is open.

Data that stays on your computer

The extension stores settings, a saved locator library, and a cached license status in Chrome’s local extension storage on your computer. That data is not uploaded to our servers. We do not operate an application backend.

Page content, URLs, and locators never leave your browser through this extension.

Data handled by payment providers

Paid plans are billed by ExtensionPay through Stripe. If you start checkout or restore a purchase, ExtensionPay and Stripe process the email address and payment details you give them. We do not receive your card number.

  • extensionpay.com/privacy
  • stripe.com/privacy

What we do not do

  • We do not show ads
  • We do not sell or share page data
  • We do not use analytics or telemetry
  • We do not run content scripts on the websites you visit

Chrome Web Store limited use

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Questions, refunds, and privacy requests: kublitskiyandrey202@gmail.com

The extension’s Account screen has Send feedback, which opens your mail app to that address. It does not attach page content, URLs, or locators.

If you uninstall the extension, Chrome deletes its local storage on that profile.
