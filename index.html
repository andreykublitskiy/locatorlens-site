Stable locators in Chrome DevTools

Rank unique CSS, XPath, and Playwright locators for Selenium, Playwright, and Cypress tests. No ads. No page data leaves the browser.

LocatorLens is a DevTools sidebar for QA engineers and developers. Select an element, or pick from the page, and it ranks locators, shows how many nodes each one matches, and warns when an id or class looks generated.

A 14-day trial includes every feature. After that, monthly, yearly, and lifetime plans are billed through Stripe via ExtensionPay. Saved locators stay on your computer. You can still export them after the trial or a subscription ends.
